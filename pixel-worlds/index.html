About

Pixel Worlds is a cozy puzzle where every cleared board reveals a beautiful hidden photo.

Tap a free tile and it flies to the tray. Three identical tiles disappear. Clear the whole board before the tray fills up, and the picture under the tiles comes into focus. Simple to learn, calm to play, and surprisingly tricky when the layers pile up.
