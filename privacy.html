Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NovaAppsStudio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile applications we publish (collectively, the "Apps"). This single policy applies to every current and future NovaAppsStudio app, including:

  • Calendar Nova
  • Quiz Nova (Galaxy Quiz)
  • Puzzle Nova: Gravity Merge
  • Pixel Worlds: Tile Match
  • Any other app published by NovaAppsStudio on Google Play that links to this page

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our Apps do not require you to create an account, do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and do not have a server-side backend. All of your app data — such as tasks, reminders, quiz progress, scores, coins, streaks, and language/settings preferences — is stored locally on your device only, using on-device storage (Hive). We never see, collect, or transmit this data to ourselves.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our free Apps display ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • General device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, coarse location derived from IP)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control what Google/AdMob does with this data beyond what is described there. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3. Permissions

Our Apps only request the Internet permission, which is required to load ads. We do not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. The only data shared is the advertising-related data described in Section 2, which is shared with Google AdMob for the sole purpose of displaying ads.

5. Data Deletion

Since all app data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's data/cache in your device's Android settings. To reset your advertising identifier, use your device's system settings as described in Section 2.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new apps or features. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of any NovaAppsStudio app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
