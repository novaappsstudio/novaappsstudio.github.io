About

Welcome to Shoo Zoo, a cozy logic puzzle where every tap sends a happy animal running home.

The paddock is full of animals, and each one looks in its own direction. Tap an animal: if the way in front of it is clear, it runs along the path to the barn. If someone is in the way, it bumps into them and you lose a heart. Find the right order, free everyone and earn up to 3 stars.

Features

  • 🐄Fields full of animalsMeadow, Farm, Forest, Jungle, Desert, Arctic, Sea and more on the way
  • 🐾Lots of cute animalsFrom cows and sheep to penguins, octopuses, lions and giraffes
  • 🎃Obstacles that make you thinkPumpkins, stumps, stones, ice and more
  • 💣Bomb levelsFree the animal with the bomb before the counter runs out
  • ⏱️Timer levelsFree the whole field before time is up
  • 🔥Expert fieldThe biggest boards, the most obstacles, double rewards
  • 📅Daily QuestA fresh puzzle every day with extra Paws

  • 🧑‍🌾Avatars and rewardsUnlock avatars and fill your wooden reward board
