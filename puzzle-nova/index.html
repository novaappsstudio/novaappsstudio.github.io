About

Puzzle Nova is a space puzzle where planets merge into stars.

Swipe to move every object at once. Two identical objects merge into a bigger one: dust becomes a comet, a comet becomes a moon, a moon a planet, and planets are born into stars, novas and even galaxies. Unlike classic 2048, there is no randomness: every level is a handcrafted puzzle with a proven optimal solution.

Features

  • 🌌Unique galaxiesFrom Moon Orbit to Nova Core
